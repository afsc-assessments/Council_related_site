GOA and BSAI catch and harvest specifications

Annual groundfish catch histories, ABC, TAC, and OFL, with data downloads and source coverage.

The Gulf of Alaska (GOA) and Bering Sea and Aleutian Islands (BSAI) series below bring together realized catch and harvest specifications. Acceptable biological catch (ABC), total allowable catch (TAC), and the overfishing limit (OFL) describe management quantities; realized catch describes removals recorded by the source.

Explore the time series

Choose a source, region, and year range. Use Choose species or groups to select one or more species, and choose ABC, TAC, or OFL for harvest specifications. Each selected species is drawn separately. Hover over a point for its value and coverage; drag across the plot to zoom.

AKFIN provides broad species groups and labels catch preliminary. NOAA species totals combine their area, gear, sector, and CDQ accounts. Harvest specifications retain the source’s species labels and partial-value flags. Species and management group definitions vary over time and between sources; the explorer keeps the sources separate. Open circles identify sums with missing source entries, and gaps identify missing values.

The BSAI source compilation includes overlapping sablefish regional subtotals and area components for ABC and TAC in 1995–1996. The plot retains those source sums and flags the affected sablefish and all-groundfish values; use the source-row notes when interpreting them.

Regional catch history, 2003–2025

The public AKFIN Economic SAFE report GFSAFE001 supplies the full 2003–2025 catch history available through that report for both regions. The source labels these data preliminary and reports a refresh date of 15 September 2026. Values include retained and discarded FMP groundfish catch, including groundfish caught while targeting halibut. The catch definition follows the source throughout this series.

The 2025 values are 1,794,251 metric tons for BSAI and 245,152 metric tons for GOA. These are the AKFIN series values. NOAA CAR110 totals, described below, use a different set of catch accounts.

Harvest-specification history, from 1986

The ABC_TAC source project supplies 1986–2026 BSAI and 1986–2024 GOA records. This portal uses the full available history from those two source files at the linked revision. BSAI records use lag = 1 and OY = 1; GOA also uses Area = Total, which avoids adding area subtotals to their regional totals. The year is the year to which a specification applies.

Specifications PNG · Specifications SVG · Full annual specifications table

Open circles identify sums with missing source entries. For example, the source contains one zero and 28 missing OFL entries for BSAI in 1991; the displayed zero preserves that source record and its partial coverage. This compilation supports examination of the historical series; regulatory totals and current limits should be checked against the cited NOAA records.

Missing source entries remain missing. Available entries are summed, and each downloadable annual record reports how many entries contributed and how many were missing. Partial sums therefore describe the available source records. Source row identifiers preserve repeated species/area labels for review. Early all-missing fields remain blank. The source’s zero values remain in the downloadable data with their coverage notes.

The GOA source ends in 2024; later GOA specifications require an update from the NOAA harvest-specification records. The 2026 BSAI specifications describe limits for an ongoing fishing year. All catch series on this page end in 2025.

NOAA quota-account totals, 2013–2025

NOAA CAR110 annual catch reports provide a separate series for each region through 31 December. The parser reads each report’s published Total Catch grand total once and checks it against the sum of its area/account rows. All 26 region-year reports pass the full-year and duplicate-account checks; rounding differences are at most six metric tons.

The 2025 CAR110 totals are 1,774,231 metric tons for BSAI, including Community Development Quota (CDQ) catch, and 231,545 metric tons for GOA. The GOA report includes fixed-gear and trawl sablefish accounts; adding separate IFQ sablefish landings would count those catches twice. Squid accounts appear through 2018 and sculpin accounts through 2020. Subsequent NOAA ecosystem-component reports hold those categories separately.

AKFIN and CAR110 totals remain separate throughout the figures and downloads. Their differences reflect source coverage and revision dates. Older historical summaries use additional definitions and rounding conventions, so the 2003 start remains explicit.

Sources and reproducibility

Series Source and checks Rebuild code

ABC_TAC harvest specifications Annual sum checks · Selected source rows · Repeated labels · Pinned input files and checksums R data, figure, and table builder

The public website repository includes the input snapshots under data/source/. Download that ABC_TAC directory with its data/, scripts/, R/, and doc/ layout intact. Run the parsers from the archived inputs, followed by the R scripts. Each parser checks the source-file checksums before rebuilding. The Python parsers’ --refresh option retrieves new catch-report versions; the specification builder retrieves its pinned public Git revision. A later source revision requires a reviewed data update.

The data update verified 552 AKFIN table rows and 1,808 NOAA area/account rows. AKFIN species sums agree with its reported totals within two metric tons, and the two regions sum to the Alaska total within one metric ton. The harvest-specification checks reproduce 225 available measure sums from the source report and preserve 15 all-missing sums as missing.

This update prepares data summaries and figures. The linked ABC/TAC report retains its own statistical analyses, results, and model-validation status.
